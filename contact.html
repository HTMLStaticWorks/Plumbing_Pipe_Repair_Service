COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions our clients ask about our plumbing services.

Do you offer 24/7 emergency service?

Yes, we provide round-the-clock emergency plumbing services. Our dispatch team is always ready to send a licensed technician to your home or business, even on weekends and holidays.

Are your plumbers licensed and insured?

Absolutely. Every plumber on our team is fully licensed, bonded, and insured. We conduct rigorous background checks and continuous training to ensure top-quality service and your peace of mind.

Do you provide free estimates?

We provide upfront, flat-rate pricing before any work begins. For larger projects like full pipe replacements or remodeling, we offer free on-site consultations and detailed estimates.

What areas do you serve?

We proudly serve the entire Metro City area and all surrounding neighborhoods within a 35-mile radius. If you're unsure whether you fall in our service area, please give us a call.
